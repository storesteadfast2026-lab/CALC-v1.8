### Automated operational quote test

Created:

- `app/apps/freight/services/operational_test_runner.py`
- `app/apps/freight/management/commands/run_operational_quote_tests.py`
- `app/apps/freight/tests/test_operational_test_runner.py`

Modified:

- `README.md`

The runner:

- reads legacy `.xls` dispatch data with `xlrd`;
- filters for the requested active client and eligible rows;
- selects one deterministic historical row per unique suburb/state, distributed state-by-state;
- resolves the postcode only from the operational `Suburb` table and does not invent a postcode when it is missing or ambiguous;
- uses historical total weight, cubic volume and pallet/quantity data as calculator input;
- retains the normal operational pallet tare and freight consolidation rules;
- runs the real `FreightCalculatorService` without database writes;
- records complete, provisional and no-quote outcomes, warnings, operational source versions, rate paths and structural calculation checks;
- does not use historical price differences as a pass/fail criterion;
- writes JSON, CSV and Markdown reports to `reports/operational_tests`.

The TEAMTAS rating-unit check was corrected during implementation: TEAMTAS uses whole tonnes/cubic rating units, so its chargeable value must not be compared directly with kilograms.

### Results for all 303 eligible destinations

- 270 of 303 destinations produced at least one quote result: 89.1 per cent.
- 33 of 303 destinations produced no quote result: 10.9 per cent.
- 48 destinations had at least one `COMPLETE` result.
- 222 destinations had results, but all their results were provisional.
- All 303 cases are currently labelled `REVIEW`; none is `PASS` and none is `FAIL`.
- The report contains 48 complete carrier/service results, 692 provisional carrier/service results, 835 `NO_QUOTE` warnings and 692 `PARTIAL` warnings. These are result/warning counts, not destination counts.
- The 835 `NO_QUOTE` entries affect 256 destinations. A destination can contain warnings for several carrier/services while still receiving a quote from another carrier/service.
- Evidence for the aggregate values: `reports/operational_tests/sth_operational_test_20261120_143357.json`, `summary` and `cases`.

### Why everything is `REVIEW`

- Current interpretation defines `PASS` as results with all structural checks passing and no warnings; `REVIEW` includes any postcode ambiguity, provisional charge or operational source warning. Evidence: `app/apps/freight/services/operational_test_runner.py:370-395` and `README.md:72-76`.
- Because the calculator evaluates several carrier/services for each destination, one carrier warning makes the entire destination `REVIEW`, even when another carrier returns a valid or complete quote. Therefore, `REVIEW = 303` does not mean zero destinations were quoted.

- The automated run validates structural execution and operational procedure behaviour; it does not prove that every commercial rate or surcharge is correct.
- Historical prices were deliberately not used as expected values. Price equality with the original workbook was not tested.
- No claim has been made that provisional estimates are commercially approved. Their unresolved charges remain visible.
- The 33 no-quote destinations and the individual warning causes have not yet been resolved.

## 6. Decisions made by the user

- The application must be independent from the original spreadsheet; the spreadsheet was a development base, not a continuing operational dependency.
- Operational files and application-owned tables must drive the live procedure for STH.
- Changing rate values are expected; automated tests should validate the procedure and business-rule sequence rather than require prices to remain equal.
- Missing operational values should be warned about and resolved later, not fabricated.
- Rates and Zones should replace the STH operational data only through their validated workflow; Stock is informational and must not block quotes.
- Product rows without a client must not be assigned to STH merely to make them load; omit or remove those rows from the STH import scope.
- Ambiguous Zones should produce a warning and be resolved by telephone or later business review.
- Customer-facing unresolved messages should direct users to Steadfast Customer Service.
- The test must be runnable independently from Docker to avoid repeatedly using this chat.
- The historical test scope is one case for each unique locality, initially sampled at 100 and then run over all eligible STH localities.
- Australian English is required in the application and documents; Spanish is only the developer's discussion language.
- The reporting must not confuse “quoted” with “requires review”.
